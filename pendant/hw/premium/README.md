# Premium power: circuit draft for review only

**Do not connect a real primary cell or hybrid capacitor (including Vina VPC and Eaton HSH/HSL).** Bare-board [prototype fabrication files](fab/README.md) are available for evaluation, but this electrical draft is not qualified for real-cell operation. D1 = BAT41ZFILM and R6 = 1.2 kΩ 1% are **emulator-only screening candidates**, not qualified primary-cell protection. They are placed before every primary branch including BQ25505 VBAT_PRI, consistent with Panasonic's [primary-cell caution](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/AAA4000/AAA4000COL15.pdf). Check the exact cell's reverse charge and fault requirements, leakage, forward drop, surge and operating temperatures. LM66100 U4 does not replace that requirement. Confirm diode-short, reversed-cell and failure current limits.

The demo evaluation target is a single CR2032 backup cell for 24 hours. SMD components are on the front; provisional through-hole footprints put the CH005-2032LF holder and horizontal Vina capacitor side by side on the back, with their leads hand-soldered from the front. Bare test pads are also on the back and have been moved outside their approximate courtyards. Pad-in-via is accepted for home assembly, subject to solder wicking and insulation checks. The user reported physical checks of the holder and capacitor on 2026-11-06; individual measured values were not supplied. Enclosure interference, test-pad access and backup runtime remain unverified. The selected first evaluation panel is the uxcell round 36 mm candidate, whose actual Voc, MPP and thickness have not been measured. R3/R4/R5 now use 787 kΩ / 6.34 MΩ / 4.87 MΩ, 0.1%, for nominal falling/rising VBAT_OK of 2.785 / 2.981 V. This gives the Vina 30 F's 2.5 V operating floor more static margin than the prior draft; it does not protect against self-discharge in storage or establish dynamic margin. It also reduces available energy before switching. See the design calculation and bench conditions. The new divider remains an evaluation candidate, not evidence that the schematic is safe or complete.

The candidate source paths are `VSTOR → U3 LM66100 → Q1 common-source back-to-back → VOR`, and `cell emulator → D1 → R6 → COIN → U4 LM66100 → Q2 common-source back-to-back → VOR`. BQ VBAT_PRI is sensed at COIN, downstream of D1/R6. Both LM66100 comparators use CE=their own VOUT. A LM66100 permits transient and small reverse current and has temperature-dependent leakage. Neither the primary cell nor the secondary energy path has been proven safe, and all startup and switching states require bench measurement with emulators.

For C1/C3/C5/C6, 10 µF 16 V and 0805 are provisional, with [Yageo CC0805KRX5R7BB106](https://www.yageogroup.com/download/specsheet/CC0805KRX5R7BB106) as a candidate. Actual capacitors still need bias, tolerance, temperature and leakage checks; the BQ solar input and VSTOR each require ≥4.7 µF **effective**. C8 is an intentionally unselected primary-side reservoir after D1/R6; its 1210 footprint is a placement placeholder, not a capacitance selection. Its value, actual size, leakage and failure effects are not validated, and VOR holdup is also not sized. J1 has provisional solder-wire pads; J2 and J3 use evaluation footprints for the horizontal Vina capacitor and CH005-2032LF holder. On 2026-11-06 the user reported checking both physical parts, including terminal polarity, hole locations and lead bends; measured values were not recorded. L1's Coilcraft land pattern has been compared with [LPS4018](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/31b05c96-4c4c-4498-8dbb-fe092de76bad/lps4018.pdf). L2 now uses a local footprint with nominal pad dimensions following the [DFE201612E recommended pattern](https://elektronik.ropla.eu/pdf/stock/mur/dfe201612eseries.pdf); assembly remains untested.

The local `premium.pretty` library contains provisional TI RGR0020A and CSD75208W1015 footprints. BQ's external copper pads and four exposed-pad paste openings follow the example dimensions in the [BQ25505 package drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25505.pdf). The CSD top-view copper pattern follows [TI §7.2](https://www.ti.com/lit/gpn/csd75208w1015), but **its F.Paste is intentionally absent**: a stencil generated as-is will have no paste openings for Q1/Q2. Determine the stencil and solder-mask design with the assembler before fabrication. Both footprints still need manufacturing and assembly verification, including BQ thermal vias if used. NAND's 8 × 6 mm / 1.27 mm pitch and nominal 3.4 × 4.3 mm exposed pad match the [F35UQA512M package drawing](https://cdn.icstop.com/upload/pdfs/e7/de/e7decc6bb7e9f2384da286e27083f166.pdf), but its EP electrical treatment and stencil need confirmation.

BQ NC pins 6/16/17 and exposed pad connect to GND. U2 is the provisional TPS62842DGRR, selected from the TPS62840 family because the smaller TPS62840DLCR was unavailable from the checked distributor. Its DGR package is larger and **not pin-compatible** with DLC: VSET pin 4 = GND selects 1.8 V, pin 7 is NC, and exposed pad 9 = GND. The unused DLC STOP function has no DGR pin. The provisional KiCad generic footprint has 0.50 mm outer pad width versus 0.30 mm in TI's land example; EP mask and paste also differ. Confirm the selected footprint and stencil with the assembler, along with output accuracy, effective capacitance and transients before PCB release. [TI device comparison, Table 1 and package example](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62840.pdf).

U5 is the SN74LVC1G17DBVR evaluation buffer: DBV pin 1 NC, 2 A from VBAT_OK_RAW, 3 GND, 4 Y to VBAT_OK_1V8, 5 VCC=1V8, with C7 100 nF. Its output goes to U103 B2/P0.03; TP12 is raw VBAT_OK and must not connect to a 1.8 V GPIO. Verify input thresholds, leakage, rail-off back-power, the input state when VSTOR is absent but the primary source powers 1V8, and the energy budget before adoption. [TI pinout and Ioff](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g17.pdf). No HSL/COIN voltage dividers are connected: a divider alone must not energize a powered-off MCU through its ADC. P1.05/AIN1 is reserved but unconnected.

## Demo shared core and measurements

The sheet includes the BC15M U103, F35UQA512M U105, T5848 U108, microphone switch/buffer, button and SWD pogo from the Standard definitions. The optional external RTC and terminal-indicator circuits are omitted for this demo; no claim is made that the omitted product functions work. SW101 is a **bridged solder jumper**, not an operable privacy latch. Select and validate the actual physical control and check mic SD/WAKE back-power and isolation.

For the demo, NAND shares 1V8 through 0 Ω R106 and is **not independently power-gated**. This avoids the previous steady state in which a powered MCU could drive an unpowered NAND's pins, and removes the load switch and EN pulldown. It does not prove ramp or signal-transition behavior. The NAND datasheet gives 10 µA typ / 50 µA max standby under its stated conditions: account for that separately, rather than reusing a gated-NAND energy estimate. NAND remains powered on the coin source; firmware must separately prevent unqualified writes, erases, BLE and OTA. Before opening R106 for current measurement, de-energize and connect the meter without leaving a powered MCU driving an unpowered NAND; verify the meter's burden and all transitions.

R7 is the 1V8 main measurement link, R8 is the CAP measurement link; TP1–TP12 expose SOLAR, BQ-side HSL, VSTOR, protected COIN, VOR, 1V8_BUCK, SEC_GATE, PRI_GATE, CAP_EXT, MIC_EN, VBAT_OK_1V8 and VBAT_OK_RAW respectively. TP104/105/106/107 expose 1V8, NAND_1V8, MIC_1V8 and GND; the core also retains UART, marker and MIC_1V8_PRE points. J1 is a provisional solar interface; J2 and J3 use provisional physical capacitor and holder footprints whose mechanics and polarity require verification. The test fixture must not bypass the primary protection or become a USB supply path in the demo.

Symbols are pin-labelled boxes with IC pin electrical types; MOSFET conduction pins are passive. Signal names are local labels. KiCad 10 ERC reports **two errors, zero warnings**: U3/U4 open-collector ST tied to GND, as directed by the [LM66100 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm66100.pdf) when unused. This KiCad rule conflict is deliberately not suppressed. ERC does not validate protection or select missing values. The generator's netlist comparison checks its own map; `check_netlist.py` additionally checks selected critical source, pin and rail invariants against the exported KiCad netlist. Neither test proves electrical safety. After adding a GND via, the 2026-11-06 PCB DRC reports zero unconnected items and zero errors; see [PCB draft status and layout blockers](PCB_DRAFT.md) for warnings and physical checks. The prototype must not be powered with real cells before those protections are qualified.

Before any physical evaluation, review the emulator connections, voltage/current limits and source states; the solar input cannot always be modeled by a low-impedance bench supply. Finalizing a manufacturing PCB remains blocked on the unresolved items above and in the design gate; PCB placement and routing revision, mechanical evaluation and independent evaluation-fixture preparation can proceed in parallel.

## Primary-series resistor screening (not a design value)

The following CR1632 arithmetic is a historical screening example; it does not select R6 for the CR2032. The 2002 [Panasonic coin-cell caution, p. 12](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/AAA4000/AAA4000COL15.pdf) lists abnormal charge currents of 4 mA for CR1632 and 10 mA for CR2032. With 3.8 V across the series resistor, 1.2 kΩ 1% has a 1.188 kΩ minimum, giving approximately 3.20 mA. This is a screening calculation, not proof of safe charging or an approved choice. An illustrative reversed 3 V cell plus a 3.8 V driven COIN node gives 6.8 V across a shorted D1/R6 path: approximately 5.72 mA and 38.9 mW in R6. This is a different fault condition and is not directly comparable to the cited charge-current figure. Evaluate negative IC-pin voltages, actual maximum voltage, resistor temperature/fault/power ratings, diode-short, duration and cumulative charge.

The [BAT41ZFILM](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/bat41.pdf) specifies ≤0.45 V forward drop at 1 mA, 25 °C, pulsed, and reverse leakage ≤100 nA at 50 V, 25 °C. These are not guaranteed values at every operating condition. For perspective only, an assumed 3.0 V cell, 0.45 V diode drop and R6 = 1.212 kΩ leave at most about 0.45 mA before COIN drops to the BQ's 2.0 V recommended minimum, even before cell ESR and other losses. C8 must supply the missing load energy and then recover through R6. Real-cell connection is prohibited until reverse leakage/charge, fault protection, bulk, startup and transition measurements are resolved.
